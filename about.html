Area and accessibility

Getting there

The festival is on an old farmyard by the lake in Alavus. Festival buses run from the town centre on both days.

Accessibility

  • Accessible parking near the entrance
  • Step-free route to all three stages
  • Accessible toilets
  • Quiet rest area by the lake
