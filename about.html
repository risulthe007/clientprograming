Area and accessibility

Getting there

The festival is held on an old farmyard by the lake in Alavus. Festival buses run from the town centre during both days.

The area

There are three stages, food stalls and a lakeside rest area.

Accessibility

  • Accessible parking close to the entrance
  • Step-free route to all three stages
  • Accessible toilets in the festival area
  • A quiet rest area by the lake
