Mood board: Pohjanpiha Festival

Customer: Noora Lehtimäki, festival producer.

Feeling

Bold, summery, clear.

Bold: it is a music festival and the brief asks for big, bold type, so the headings are very thick and big.
Summery: the festival is in July by a lake. Navy is like the lake, the light background is like the sky and coral is like the sunset.
Clear: people check the lineup on the bus or in the crowd, so the lineup is simple tables with the time first. There are no images, so the site is fast even with a weak signal.

Colours

Navy #1b2a4a: text, header, footer
Light blue-white #f4f6fb: background
Coral #e0583b: lines under headings, menu, focus outline
Dark coral #b8432a: buttons and links
Dark navy #0f1830: background in dark mode
Light coral #ff9a80: links in dark mode

Contrast checks (4.5 : 1 needed)

Navy text on light background: 13.2 : 1, ok
White on navy header: 14.2 : 1, ok
White on coral #e0583b: 3.7 : 1, not ok
White on dark coral #b8432a: 5.4 : 1, ok
Light text on dark navy (dark mode): 16.3 : 1, ok
Dark navy text on coral (dark mode button): 4.7 : 1, ok
Light coral link on dark navy: 8.5 : 1, ok

I first wanted white text on the coral buttons, but I checked it and it was only 3.7 : 1. So I made a darker coral for buttons in light mode. In dark mode the button is the normal coral with dark text, and that passes.

Fonts

Headings: Arial Black, fallback system-ui. Big and bold like the brief says.
Text: system-ui, the phone's own font.

I did not use Google Fonts because the site has to be fast (last year people gave up on the bus) and Google Fonts sends the visitor's IP to Google, which is a GDPR problem.

Layout

Mobile first. On a 320 px phone everything is in one column.
On a bigger screen (48rem) Day 1 and Day 2 are side by side, the home page boxes are in a row and the Tickets button goes to the right.
Dark mode follows the phone or computer setting.
The page you are on has a coral line under it in the menu.
